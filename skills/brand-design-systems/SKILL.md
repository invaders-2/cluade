# Brand Design Systems

本 skill 提供 73 个知名品牌的完整设计系统规范，文件存放在 `~/.claude/references/brand-designs/` 目录下。

## 使用方式

当需要按某个品牌风格设计时：

1. 用 Read 工具读取对应品牌的 DESIGN.md 文件，例如 `~/.claude/references/brand-designs/apple/DESIGN.md`
2. 按照规范中的颜色、排版、组件等规则来设计

airbnb, airtable, apple, binance, bmw, bmw-m, bugatti, cal, claude, clay, clickhouse, cohere, coinbase, composio, cursor, dell-1996, elevenlabs, expo, ferrari, figma, framer, hashicorp, hp, ibm, intercom, kraken, lamborghini, linear.app, lovable, mastercard, meta, minimax, mintlify, miro, mistral.ai, mongodb, nike, notion, nvidia, ollama, opencode.ai, pinterest, playstation, posthog, raycast, renault, replicate, resend, revolut, runwayml, sanity, sentry, shopify, slack, spacex, spotify, starbucks, stripe, supabase, superhuman, tesla, theverge, together.ai, uber, vercel, vodafone, voltagent, warp, webflow, wired, wise, x.ai, zapier

每个文件夹内含 DESIGN.md，包含：视觉主题、色板、排版、组件、布局、阴影、响应式等完整规范。
